# 🤖 JARVIS AI - Real Life 3D Assistant

A fully functional, 3D Anime AI assistant for your Windows PC. Jarvis can walk in, talk to you, see your screen, analyze files, and control your computer using voice or text commands!

## ✨ Features
- **3D Avatar:** Built with Three.js, complete with walking, breathing, blinking, and lip-sync animations.
- **Full PC Control:** Can open apps, manage files, and execute PowerShell commands.
- **Vision & File Analysis:** Can "see" your screen and read documents.
- **Emotional Voice:** Dynamic voice pitch and rate based on her mood (Happy, Serious, Sad).
- **Long-term Memory:** Remembers facts about you across restarts.

## 🛠️ Prerequisites
You must have **[Node.js](https://nodejs.org/)** installed on your computer.

## 🚀 Quick Start (One-Click)
1. Download or Clone this repository.
2. Double-click the **`setup.bat`** file.
3. Wait a few seconds for the terminal to install dependencies and launch the browser.
4. When the webpage opens, click the **🔑 API Key** button in the top left.
5. Paste your **Groq API Key** (Get a free one at [console.groq.com](https://console.groq.com/keys)).
6. Click **SAVE**. 

She will greet you and is ready to obey your commands!

## 🎮 How to use
- **Talk:** Click the Microphone icon and speak, or type in the text box.
- **Look at Screen:** Click `👁️ Look at Screen` and select the window you want her to see.
- **Analyze Files:** Click `📁 Analyze File` to let her read text, code, or look at images.
- **Commands to try:**
  - *"Open Notepad"*
  - *"Create a folder called Projects on my desktop"*
  - *"What is my IP address?"*
  - *"Tell me a joke"*

## 📁 Note on 3D Model
Ensure your 3D model file is named **`avatar.glb`** and placed in the root directory. If you do not have one, you can download free `.vrm` or `.glb` anime models from [VRoid Hub](https://hub.vroid.com/).
